# Leverage: what to look for, what it's worth

Leverage is arithmetic you can show the seller. Anything you cannot put a
kroner figure on is not leverage, it is complaining.

## The dominant factor: your alternatives

Negotiators with a strong BATNA make the first offer more often and do better
when they do. Everything below is a rounding error compared to genuinely
having somewhere else to go.

Before drafting anything, count the comparable listings. Write the count down.

| Comparable cars listed | Position | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| 10+ | Strong | Full ladder, hard opening, walk freely |
| 4-9 | Workable | Compressed ladder, cite specific rivals |
| 1-3 | Weak | Open near target, move fast, prioritise getting the car |
| 1 | Very weak | Do not posture. Negotiate politely on documented defects only |

The instinct to negotiate harder when the car is rare is exactly backwards.

## Days on market

The single most reliable dealer-side lever. Dealers finance stock on
floorplan and pay interest while it sits.

| Days listed | Dealer state | Realistic discount |
|---|---|---|
| < 30 | Fresh, firm | 0-2% |
| 30-60 | Softening | 2-5% |
| 60-90 | Aged | 5-8% |
| 90+ | Stale, costing money | 8-12% |

On finn.no, read "Sist endret" on the listing page. It is not the publication
date, but a listing edited long ago has sat long. A listing whose price has
been edited downward has a seller already conceding to the market, which is
the moment to arrive.

Private sellers have no floorplan cost, so time pressure works differently on
them: it is life events, not interest. Ask why they are selling.

## Price against mileage

Compute kr/km spread across the comparable set. A car priced like a low-km
example while carrying high km is the clearest argument you can make, because
it is the seller's own market disagreeing with them.

Present it as a table, not a sentence. Numbers in a column are harder to
argue with than numbers in prose.

## Documentation gaps

Each of these is a real cost or a real risk, and each converts to kroner:

| Gap | Ask for | Worth if missing |
|---|---|---|
| No service history | Full stamped record | 5 000-15 000 |
| No battery SOH report | Test at BYD workshop | 10 000-20 000 |
| No EU-kontroll date | Next due date | Cost of the test plus likely repairs |
| Unclear ownership count | Vognkort | Risk, not price |
| Import (not Norwegian-delivered) | Documentation | 10 000-25 000, resale is harder |

An EV without a battery report is the strongest documented ask you have. It is
cheap for the seller to obtain, so refusal is itself informative.

## Physical condition

Inspect or have inspected. NAF's condition test exists precisely for this and
costs a fraction of what it finds.

| Item | Threshold | Cost to you |
|---|---|---|
| Tyres | under 4 mm tread | 8 000-12 000 for a set |
| Winter tyres | not included | 8 000-15 000 |
| Brake discs | lipped or corroded | 6 000-12 000 |
| 12V battery | over 4 years old | 2 000-3 000 |
| Charge cable | Type 2 missing | 3 000-5 000 |
| Body damage | any | Get a quote, then deduct the quote |

EVs eat brake discs through corrosion rather than wear, because regenerative
braking means the friction brakes are rarely used. NAF flags this specifically
for used EVs. Always look at the discs.

## Market timing

- **End of month and quarter.** Dealer sales targets. Real, and worth timing
  a final offer around.
- **Model facelift.** A superseded model year loses value quickly and the
  seller knows it.
- **Seasonal.** Norwegian used prices soften in autumn and stiffen in spring.
- **Tax changes.** Norwegian EV tax rules have moved repeatedly. A change
  taking effect at year-end pulls sellers into the market before it and
  buyers into the market after it.

## Assembling the argument

One message. A short table. One number. Then stop.

> Sammenlignet med de andre 2024-ene som ligger ute nå:
>
> | Bil | Km | Pris |
> |---|---|---|
> | Din | 27 900 | 299 000 |
> | [finnkode] | 10 250 | 309 000 |
> | [finnkode] | 12 193 | 299 500 |
>
> Bilen din har nesten tre ganger kilometerstanden til de to andre, men ligger
> på samme pris. Jeg kan tilby [tall] kr, betaling denne uken.

The table does the work. Do not add adjectives to it.
